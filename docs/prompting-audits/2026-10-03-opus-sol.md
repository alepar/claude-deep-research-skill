## Systemic patterns

### Rigid output size and proxy metrics replace editorial judgment (score 9.7)

Word quotas, finding counts, bullet counts, paragraph ratios, and inherited continuation averages make the report conform to a preset shape even when the question and evidence need another one. Opus is especially prone to filling the available space, while the shared template also presses Sol toward list-heavy output.

- **Fix once:** Keep required report sections and claim-level evidence rules, but size findings and sections by reader, decision, and evidentiary weight; move any genuinely useful measurements to optional code diagnostics rather than prose gates.
